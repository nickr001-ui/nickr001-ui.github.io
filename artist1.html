Neon Trees

About Neon Trees

Neon Trees is an American alternative rock band formed in Provo, Utah, in 2005. The band was started by childhood friends and neighbors Tyler Glenn (lead vocals and keyboards) and Chris Allen (lead guitar), who later completed the iconic four-piece lineup by inviting bassist Branden Campbell and drummer/vocalist Elaine Bradley to join. The band took their name from the glowing neon palm tree signs found at California's In-N-Out Burger restaurants.

After releasing independent EPs and building a fiery reputation in the Utah underground indie music scene, Neon Trees received their big national break in 2008 when they were chosen to open for The Killers during their North American tour. This exposure led to a contract with Mercury Records, leading to the release of their debut full-length studio album, Habits, in March 2010.

The lead single from Habits, titled "Animal," became an incredible crossover hit. It reached Number 1 on the Billboard Alternative Songs chart and climbed into the top 20 of the Billboard Hot 100. The song was certified double platinum in the United States and won Top Rock Song at the 2011 Billboard Music Awards. Neon Trees continued their success with subsequent multi-platinum pop-rock anthems like "Everybody Talks" (from 2012's Picture Show) and "Sleeping with a Friend" (from 2014's Pop Psychology), cementing their legacy as one of the defining dance-rock acts of the 2010s.
